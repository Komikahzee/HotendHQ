Volumetric flow & maximum speed

Your hot end has a melting ceiling in mm³/s. This tells you the fastest you can print before you are extruding under-melted plastic.

Stock lined hot end ≈ 11–13 · good all-metal ≈ 18–24 · high-flow ≈ 28–40. Use about 60 % of a manufacturer's headline figure.
