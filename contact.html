Support FAQ

Do the generators cost anything?

No. Every generator and guide on Hotend HQ is free to use, and the models you make are yours to print.

A generator is blank or won't load. What should I try?

Refresh the page, then try an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. The generators build models in your browser, so an old browser, a very old phone or a strict content blocker can stop them. If it still won't load, email us with your browser and device.

My exported STL looks wrong in my slicer.

Check the units are millimetres and the scale is 100%. If the model still looks broken, email us the generator name and the settings you used and we'll reproduce it.

How do I check on a print order?

Reply to the quote email you received, or email us with the name and email address you ordered with.

What happens to what I send you?

Your message is only used to answer you. We never sell or share your email address. See Privacy for the details.
