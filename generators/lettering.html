    Printing this model

      How it works

      Three tools in one

      1. Pick the tool. A tracing template, a letter stamp or a calligraphy guide ruler.
      2. Type the text or set the proportions. Choose a font and size for templates and stamps, or the x-height, nib width, ascenders, descenders and slant for a guide.
      3. Fine-tune it. Cut-out, grooved or raised letters; stamp shape, border and handle; slot width, rows and a nib ladder.
      4. Print and use. Everything prints flat without supports.
      Printing it

      Settings that work

      Stamps

      Print the letters at 0.12 mm layers for clean edges. TPU letters glued onto a PLA handle give the softest print.

      Guide rulers

      Keep slots at least 0.8 mm wide for a 0.5 mm pencil. PETG rulers stay flat on paper.

      Templates for kids

      Grooved letters guide a stylus or pencil tip; raised letters are great for tracing with a finger.

      Questions

      Lettering & calligraphy FAQ

      Are stamp letters mirrored?

      Yes, automatically, so what you stamp reads the right way round. The top of the handle shows the text unmirrored.

      How do I set up a guide for my nib?

      Choose "Nib widths", enter your nib width and how many nib widths the x-height should be. The nib ladder in the margin shows the steps.

      What slant angle should I use?

      Copperplate is about 55°, italic 5–10° from upright (80–85°). Presets are included for common hands.

      Do letters with islands work in a cut-out template?

      Yes. Bridges keep the middles of letters like O and A in place.
