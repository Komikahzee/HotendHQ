    Printing this model

      How it works

      A set of pieces in four steps

      1. Pick the piece. Tokens and coins, pawns, meeples and figures, cubes and bits, or card stands.
      2. Shape and size it. Choose the shape, size, rounding and style, like a stackable coin or a weighted pawn.
      3. Set up players. How many players, how many pieces each, and a colour for every player.
      4. Print the set. Pieces are laid out on your bed. The 3MF keeps each player's pieces as their own object.
      Printing it

      Settings that work

      Tokens with two colours

      Raised faces can take a filament change at the height shown; engraved faces can be filled with paint.

      Heavier pawns

      Use the weight pocket underneath and press in a coin, nut or steel ball for a satisfying weight.

      Card stands

      Measure your card or board with calipers. The fit setting sets how tightly the slot grips it.

      Questions

      Board game pieces FAQ

      Can tokens have different numbers?

      Yes. Numbers count up automatically from any start value and step, or give each token its own label from a list.

      How many players are supported?

      Up to eight, each with its own colour.

      Can I stack the coins?

      Turn on "Stackable" and each coin gets a recess underneath that the one below sits into.

      Do the pieces need supports?

      No. Everything is placed flat side down.
