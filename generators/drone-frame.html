      How it works

      A frame in four steps

      1. Choose the aircraft. Quad, X8 coaxial cinelifter, tricopter with a tilting tail, Y6, hexacopter or octocopter, from 65 mm whoops to 10-inch long range. Or start from a preset or press Randomize.
      2. Shape it. Frame shape, wheelbase, one-piece or bolt-on arms, arm shape and cutouts, motor guards, body shape and plates.
      3. Fit your parts. Motor bolt pattern, stack size and rotation, camera size and angle, standoffs, battery strap, GPS pad, antenna and action camera mounts.
      4. Check it and print. Look it over built up with motors, props and a battery, switch to the print layout, and download. It checks your props clear each other and the camera fits between the plates.
      Printing it

      Settings that work

      Material

      PA-CF or PETG-CF (carbon-filled) are the best printed frame materials; PETG is a good start. TPU is great for whoop ducts, bumpers and camera mounts.

      Strength

      Print flat with 4–6 walls and 50–100% infill. Printed arms are thicker than carbon ones to make up for the softer plastic. Bolt-on arms let you swap a broken one in minutes.

      Big frames

      Hexacopters, octocopters and 7-inch frames are bigger than most beds, so pick bolt-on arms: each part then fits, and the parts print a plate at a time.

      Questions

      Drone frames FAQ

      Can a printed frame really fly?

      Yes, especially whoops, toothpicks, cinewhoops and 3–5-inch builds. Bigger frames fly well too, but they are heavier and flexier than carbon fibre.

      Are the motors, props and battery in the download?

      No. They are only in the preview so you can see the finished drone. The download is just the printed parts, laid out flat for your bed.

      What does the Randomize button do?

      It rolls a complete new design: aircraft, size, frame shape, arms, cutouts, mounts and colours. Keep clicking until you like one, then fine-tune it.

      How does the prop check work?

      It measures the gap between every pair of props and warns you, with the wheelbase you need, if they come closer than your minimum.

      What is a deadcat frame?

      The front arms are swept wide and back so the props stay out of the camera view.

      How does the tricopter tail work?

      The tail motor sits on a hinged mount between two ears on the tail arm. A 9 g servo in the arm tilts it for yaw, like a real tricopter.

      Which motor pattern do I have?

      Most 2204–2207 motors use 16 × 16 mm M3; 2306 to 2809 motors use 19 × 19 mm; big 3110-class motors use 25 × 25 mm; 1103–1204 use 9 × 9 mm M2; tiny whoop motors use three holes.
