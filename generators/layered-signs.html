    Printing this model

      How it works

      A sign in four steps

      1. Write the text. A main line and an optional second line, each with its own font and size.
      2. Pick the shape. Eleven shapes, from rounded rectangles, ovals and shields to a banner or an outline cut to the letters.
      3. Stack the layers. Add a border, an outline around the letters, an icon or your own logo, and choose a colour for each layer.
      4. Print it. For a single-nozzle printer the generator lists the heights to change filament. For AMS or MMU, download the 3MF with a part per colour.
      Printing it

      Settings that work

      Colour changes

      In stacked mode each colour starts at its own height. Add an M600 or pause at each height shown under the preview and swap filament.

      Crisp letters

      Use a 0.4 mm nozzle with 0.16–0.2 mm layers. Letters taller than 1 mm stand out better from across a room.

      Hanging it

      Keyhole slots hide the screws and let the sign sit flat on the wall. Magnet pockets are sized to the magnets you enter.

      Questions

      Layered signs FAQ

      Do I need a multicolour printer?

      No. Stacked mode puts each colour on its own layer, so one nozzle and a few filament changes are all you need.

      Can I use my own logo?

      Yes. Upload a black and white image as the icon and it is traced into the sign on the letters or outline layer.

      What sizes can I make?

      The sign can fit around the text or be a fixed width and height. The generator warns you if it is bigger than your printer.

      How do I mount it?

      Pick keyhole slots, screw holes, hanging holes, magnet pockets or a separate desk stand at the angle you want.
