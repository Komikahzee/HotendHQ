About this part

    Printing it

    Settings that work for this part

    Functional, not pretty

    0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 25 % gyroid infill. Walls carry the load in a printed part far more than infill does, so add walls before you add infill.

    Holes print undersized

    Every hole is modelled at the size you set. Turn on hole horizontal expansion of +0.1 to +0.2 mm in your slicer, or drill them out.

    Material

    PLA is fine indoors. Anything in a hot car, near a heated bed or outdoors wants PETG or ASA. See the filament database.

    Questions

    Grid organiser FAQ

    How is this different from Gridfinity?

    This makes one tray sized to your drawer. Gridfinity is a modular system of baseplates and swappable bins. If you want to rearrange things later, use the Gridfinity generator.

    What cell size should I use?

    Measure the biggest item going in and add 2 to 3 mm so it lifts out easily.

    Will it fit my bed?

    The overall size is shown under "About this part", and you get a warning if it goes over 250 mm.

    Can I stack them?

    Turn on stacking feet and the trays sit on top of each other without sliding.
