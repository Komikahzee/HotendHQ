About this part

    Printing it

    Settings that work for this part

    Functional, not pretty

    0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 25 % gyroid infill. Walls carry the load in a printed part far more than infill does, so add walls before you add infill.

    Holes print undersized

    Every hole is modelled at the size you set. Turn on hole horizontal expansion of +0.1 to +0.2 mm in your slicer, or drill them out.

    Material

    PLA is fine indoors. Anything in a hot car, near a heated bed or outdoors wants PETG or ASA. See the filament database.

    Questions

    Storage box FAQ

    Will the lid fit?

    The lid is modelled with a small gap on every side, and the exact gap is listed under "About this part". It prints as a separate piece next to the box.

    What wall thickness should I use?

    Pick a multiple of your line width. With a 0.4 mm nozzle, 1.2 or 1.6 mm suits light boxes, and 2 mm or more suits boxes that get knocked around.

    How many compartments can I have?

    Up to 6 dividers each way, so up to 49 compartments. The size of each one is shown under "About this part".

    How big can it be?

    Up to 250 × 250 × 200 mm. Check that it fits your bed before you print.
