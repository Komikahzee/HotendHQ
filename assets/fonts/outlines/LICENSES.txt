Font outlines used by the Hotend HQ model generators
=====================================================

Each .json file here holds glyph outlines (Latin letters, digits and common
punctuation) converted from the font named below, so text can be turned into
3D geometry in the browser. The fonts belong to their authors and are used
under their open licences, which allow embedding and redistribution. Models
you make with them are yours; the licences do not restrict printed output.
