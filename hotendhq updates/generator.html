    Settings that work for these parts

    Functional, not pretty

    0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 25 % gyroid infill. Perimeters carry the load in a printed part far more than infill does — add walls before you add infill.

    Holes print undersized

    Every screw hole here is modelled at its nominal size. Enable hole horizontal expansion of +0.1 to +0.2 mm in your slicer, or drill them out.

    Material

    PLA is fine indoors. Anything in a hot car, near a printer's heated bed, or outdoors wants PETG or ASA — see the filament database.
